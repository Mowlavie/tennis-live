Safety & minors

Last updated October 23, 2026

Much of tennis is played by juniors, so Courtline is built around keeping young players safe.

Our rules

  • Only adults (18+) can stream, record or manage a court.
  • Teens (13–17) can watch and chat with a parent's or guardian's permission. Children under 13 can't have an account.
  • Streams are private and invite-only. Nothing is public or searchable.
  • Before going live, the streamer confirms they have permission to film everyone on camera, including parental permission for minors.
  • Chat is a group conversation that everyone in the stream can see. There are no private messages, in line with youth-sport safeguarding practices such as U.S. Center for SafeSport guidance on communication between adults and minors.
  • Never film in locker rooms, restrooms or other private areas.

Tips for parents and coaches

  • Check the tournament's or club's filming policy before you stream.
  • Share invites only with people you know.
  • Delete videos you no longer need.

Report a concern

If you see anything that worries you about a child's safety, email [safety email]. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. In the U.S. you can also report to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.
