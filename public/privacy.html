Last updated October 23, 2026

This policy explains what [Company legal name] ("Courtline") collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information: if you sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile photo. We never receive your Google password.
  • Age group and consent: whether you are 18+ or 13–17 (with parent or guardian permission), when you accepted our Terms, and the streamer's filming confirmation.
  • Videos and photos you choose to save to the cloud.
  • Chat messages sent during a live stream.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, and logs needed to run and secure the service.

Live video travels directly between devices or through our video relay provider, and is not stored unless someone records it.

How we use it

To run Courtline: sign you in, connect streams, store and show your videos to people you've invited, keep the service secure, and meet legal obligations. We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run Courtline, under contract: hosting (Render), storage and video relay (Cloudflare) and sign-in (Google). We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Children and teens

  • We don't knowingly let children under 13 create accounts or collect their personal information directly. If we learn we have, we delete it.
  • Children under 13 may appear in videos recorded by an adult who confirms they have a parent's or guardian's permission.
  • Users aged 13–17 need a parent's or guardian's permission. Parents can contact [privacy email] to review or delete their child's information.
  • Chat is group-only. There are no private messages.

How long we keep it

Videos stay until you delete them or close your account [or until the retention period of your plan ends]. Chat messages are kept [for the length of the stream / up to N days]. Logs are kept for up to [90] days.

Your choices and rights

You can delete videos in the app. You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal information by emailing [privacy email]. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU and UK), you may have additional rights, and we'll honor them as the law requires.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Stored videos are private and are opened only through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us at [security email] if you find a problem.
